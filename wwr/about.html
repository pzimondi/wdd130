Zambezi Rafting

At Zambezi Rafting, we help people enjoy the river and try something new. Our guides put safety first while making each trip fun. Bring your friends, meet our team, and make memories on the water!

History

Zambezi Rafting started in 2018 with a small group of friends who loved spending time on the river. They wanted to share that experience with others. What began with a few weekend trips grew into a company welcoming families, friends, and visitors.

Today, we still believe a good trip starts with friendly guides, teamwork, and respect for nature. Our team helps visitors prepare for the rapids, build confidence on the water, and enjoy time together outdoors.
