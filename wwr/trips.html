Choose Your Trip

River Explorer

Spend two hours getting to know the river with your guide. Start with a safety briefing and paddle practice, then enjoy a shorter route with time to take in the scenery. This is a good choice for visitors who want a shorter introduction to rafting.

Includes: Guide, raft, paddle, helmet, and life jacket.

Half Day Adventure

Make a morning of it with four hours of preparation and river time. Practise paddling as a team, tackle guided rapids, and enjoy quieter stretches between them. Bring friends and share a day that gives you something new to talk about.

Includes: Guide, rafting equipment, drinking water, and a snack.

Full Day Challenge

Set aside six hours for a longer outing with more time on the water. After your briefing, work with your guide and teammates through a varied route, with a lunch break along the way. This option suits active visitors looking for a longer rafting experience.

Includes: Guide, rafting equipment, drinking water, and lunch.

Before You Join Us

Bring swimwear, secure footwear, sunscreen, and a change of clothes. Tell our team about your rafting experience and any health concerns when you enquire. Every trip starts with a safety briefing.
