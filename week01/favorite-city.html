My Favorite City: Dubai

My favorite city is Dubai in the United Arab Emirates. What I like most is its skyline, especially the Burj Khalifa. Seeing pictures of it makes me want to visit and see how tall it really is.

I would love to explore the traditional markets, try the food, and spend some time at the beach. Going on a desert safari would also be on my list. There are so many things I would enjoy experiencing there.
